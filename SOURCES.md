# Sources — what each panel of entry.png is based on

All stories happened, or were first reported, on Tuesday 17 November 2026.
Compiled 18 November 2026 (WIB) from that day's reporting; claims that rest on a
single interested party are worded as claims in the image.

- **Lead — Crimea Bridge.** Ukrainian strike collapsed a road span of the Kerch
  Bridge; Zelenskyy called it an "unprecedented operation"; Russian missiles hit
  Kyiv's Dnipro bridges in reply. (BBC reporting via Level Headlines' Daily Brief;
  Just Security Early Edition; Zark News Daily Briefing; Open Chronicle.)
- **Hormuz tankers.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two oil tankers struck
  naval mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz; the claim was not
  independently verified. A third US carrier (USS Theodore Roosevelt) joined the
  region; Brent crude ~$92.76. (Reuters and Al Jazeera via Level Headlines and
  Zark News; Bermuda Broadcasting/BBC wire headlines.)
- **Trump in Manila.** Arrival in the Philippines for the ASEAN summit; joint
  trade statement signed with Thailand; three-day Asia trip ending in China with
  three-way Xi–Putin talks planned. (Associated Press via Level Headlines; Open
  Chronicle.)
- **Nvidia earnings.** Reported after Tuesday's close: revenue $115.8bn (+103%
  year on year, vs ~$109bn expected), data-centre revenue $107.6bn (+110%),
  adjusted EPS $2.68 vs $2.47, guidance $130bn ±3%. First report since co-founder
  Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.
  (Forbes; Gizmodo; BeInCrypto; TechDefused; Investopedia's market preview.)
- **Vulcan failure.** ULA's Vulcan launched Sunday 15 November from Cape
  Canaveral and suffered an anomaly ~10 minutes in; all 40 Amazon Leo satellites
  were lost — ULA's first total mission failure since 2006. On Tuesday ULA said
  early data suggests the first stage underperformed just after liftoff; the FAA
  grounded Vulcan. (Spaceflight Now; GeekWire; Florida Today.)
- **AI-chip loophole.** The Commerce Department moved to curb China's remote
  (cloud) access to American AI computing power / advanced chips. (Zark News;
  Investopedia; Bermuda Broadcasting/BBC wire headlines.)
- **UK sanctions & Gaza aid.** Britain sanctioned eight Israeli construction
  companies involved in West Bank settlements; the UK, Australia, Canada and
  Italy jointly urged Israel to allow rapid humanitarian aid access through
  Gaza's crossings. (Just Security Early Edition; ASAS Press World News Digest;
  Bermuda Broadcasting/BBC wire headlines.)
- **JPMorgan outage.** The bank blamed a software bug — not a cyberattack — for
  Monday's disruption to online banking, branch transactions and Zelle payments.
  (ABC News via Level Headlines.)
- **Also on Tuesday (ticker).** Pentagon failed its financial audit for the
  ninth straight year (Zark News). Japan scrambled fighter jets and protested a
  suspected Russian airspace violation (Just Security; ASAS Press). The EU
  charged Temu over illegal product sales (Bermuda Broadcasting/BBC wire).
  Flood watches/warnings along Florida's east coast (Daytona Beach
  News-Journal / National Weather Service). A severe cyclone of the north-east
  monsoon season bearing down on India's Andhra Pradesh coast (The Hindu
  BusinessLine morning report).
