2 MIDDLE EAST · ENERGY

Iran says two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz

The Guards said two tankers struck naval mines and caught fire on a route Tehran calls unauthorised — unverified. A third US carrier arrived; Brent held near $92.76.

3 MIDDLE EAST · WAR

Iran fires drones at a US radar base in Bahrain

Tehran said its drones hit a US radar system at Sheikh Isa base; Bahrain said it intercepted the attack, with no casualties. Washington called it a sharp escalation.

4 ASIA · DIPLOMACY

Trump lands in Manila for the ASEAN summit

The president arrived in the Philippines and signed a joint trade statement with Thailand, opening a three-day Asia trip ending in China with talks alongside Xi and Putin.

5 MARKETS · TECH

Nvidia beats forecasts in first report since Huang’s exit

Revenue jumped 103% to $115.8bn as data-centre sales hit $107.6bn, and Nvidia guided to $130bn next quarter. First report since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned.

6 SPACE

Vulcan rocket fails — 40 Amazon satellites lost

ULA said its Vulcan’s first stage underperformed just after Sunday’s liftoff from Cape Canaveral, dooming 40 Amazon Leo satellites. ULA’s first total failure since 2006.

7 TECH · TRADE WAR

US moves to close the AI-chip loophole for China

The Commerce Department moved to curb China’s remote access to American AI computing power, tightening rules on renting US chips through the cloud — and chip shares climbed.

8 BRITAIN · GAZA

UK sanctions settlement firms as allies press on Gaza aid

Britain sanctioned eight Israeli construction firms building settlements in the West Bank, as the UK, Australia, Canada and Italy jointly urged Israel to open Gaza’s crossings to aid.

9 UNITED STATES · BANKING

JPMorgan blames a software bug for Monday’s banking outage

The glitch slowed online banking, branch transactions and Zelle payments across the US. The bank said there was no indication the disruption was a cyberattack.

10 AMERICAS · HEALTH

US, Mexico and Bolivia lose measles-free status

PAHO said endemic transmission is back in all three after the same strain circulated for a year or more. The US held the status 26 years; 5,400+ cases in 2026, the most since 1991.

11 EUROPE · MIGRATION

Five EU states plan a Rwanda hub for deported migrants

The plan would send rejected asylum seekers to Rwanda from 2027, Euronews reported. Rights groups condemned it; the states say it will deter crossings and break the smugglers’ model.

12 AFRICA · CRIME

Seventeen killed in two mass shootings in South Africa

Police said 17 people died in separate attacks in KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng on Monday. The killings renewed a national debate over gun violence and policing in the townships.

13 AFRICA · HEALTH

Congo’s Ebola outbreak still out of control, 5,568 dead

Africa CDC said the six-month-old outbreak is spreading at a record pace, with 11,376 cases. Two experimental vaccines enter phase 2 trials; only $1.3bn of $2.9bn pledged is released.
